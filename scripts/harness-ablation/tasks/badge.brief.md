Build a `Badge` component for the component library in this repository.

A badge is a small, non-interactive label that tags a piece of content with a short category or status, for example a course topic such as "Design" or "Design Thinking" on a course page. It shows text only: no icon, and it is not clickable. It comes in two looks: a bordered one, which is the default, and a filled one.

The design is in `reference.png` at the repository root.

Deliverables:
- `packages/components/src/components/Badge/index.tsx`
- `packages/components/src/components/Badge/Badge.module.css`
- `packages/components/src/components/Badge/Badge.stories.tsx`
- Export the component and its public types from `packages/components/src/index.ts`.
